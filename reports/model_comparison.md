# Model Comparison

Generated by `src/05_evaluate_models.py`. Re-run it after changing data or features.

## Protocol
- 792 development rows are used for cross-validation and tuning; 198 holdout rows are scored once at the end.
- Repeated 5-fold CV x 3 repeats (15 folds). Reported as mean ± standard deviation across folds.
- ZIP price statistics are recomputed inside every fold from that fold's training rows only (leave-one-out for those rows), so CV has no target leakage.
- Errors are on log price. MAE of 0.24 means a typical multiplicative miss of about 27%.

## Hyperparameter search
- 40 random LightGBM configurations, each scored with the same CV folds.
- Best by CV R²: `{'num_leaves': 15, 'min_data_in_leaf': 10, 'learning_rate': 0.02, 'feature_fraction': 0.8, 'bagging_fraction': 0.7, 'lambda_l2': 5.0, 'seed': 42}` with `600` boosting rounds (everything not listed uses the defaults in `real_estate_config.py`).
- Spread across configurations: best 0.837, median 0.825, worst 0.649.
- The best CV score is slightly optimistic because it was picked as the maximum of 40 tries; the holdout column is the unbiased check.

## Caveats
- The holdout is only a few hundred rows, so differences of a few hundredths in R² are within noise.
- Only one data source and one year; no time-based validation is possible without sale dates.
